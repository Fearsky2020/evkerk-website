# Terms Skeleton — Independent Dutch Learning Product

Status: internal drafting skeleton only. Not ready for publication. Replace placeholders and obtain appropriate Dutch legal/accounting review before paid launch.

## 1. Product

`[BRAND]` provides a digital platform for practical Dutch-language learning and practice.

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, the product is not:

- an official government service;
- a formal CEFR certifying body;
- an accredited inburgering school;
- a substitute for legal, immigration, medical or professional advice.

## 2. Guest use

A learner may begin with supported guest features without creating an account.

Guest progress may be stored locally on the learner's device/browser. The learner is responsible for local browser/device deletion unless backup/sync is enabled.

## 3. Accounts

Future account rules should cover:

- accurate account contact information;
- credential security;
- one person / permitted household use where relevant;
- prohibited account resale or automated abuse;
- deletion and export rights;
- service/security communications.

## 4. Learning levels and feedback

Any in-product level label, AI recommendation, pronunciation feedback or progress indicator is a learning aid unless explicitly certified otherwise.

Do not represent automated feedback as an official educational qualification, exam result or government determination.

## 5. Free plan

The Free plan should remain genuinely useful.

The provider may change limits/features when necessary, but should avoid removing already-earned local learner data without notice or migration where reasonably possible.

## 6. Paid consumer subscriptions

Before launch, final terms must specify:

- monthly/annual price;
- VAT treatment;
- billing frequency;
- payment methods;
- automatic renewal;
- trial rules if any;
- how to cancel online;
- effective cancellation date;
- refund / cooling-off treatment for digital services;
- failed payment handling;
- plan downgrades and data retention.

Price changes should be communicated before renewal with a reasonable notice period.

## 7. AI / usage limits

Costly AI features may use fair-use or explicit usage limits.

Do not advertise unlimited AI use if the service is subject to undisclosed practical caps.

If a limit applies, show it clearly in the relevant plan description.

## 8. Institutional customers

Separate institutional terms/order form should define:

- organisation/tenant;
- number of seats or active learners;
- subscription/contract term;
- sponsored access rules;
- admin roles;
- reporting scope;
- data controller/processor roles;
- DPA;
- SLA if sold;
- support level;
- offboarding/export/deletion;
- custom content ownership and reuse rights;
- invoice/payment terms.

## 9. Employer / sector content

Custom Dutch-for-work packs should distinguish:

- customer-provided confidential content;
- reusable generic language-learning content;
- customer branding;
- ownership/licensing of custom materials;
- accuracy review responsibility for safety-critical workplace phrases.

## 10. Third-party media and open source

The product may link/embed third-party public learning media and use open-source software.

Do not claim ownership over third-party videos, transcripts or copyrighted course material.

Current open-source/license notes remain documented separately.

## 11. Availability

No consumer release should promise perfect uptime.

Institutional SLA commitments, if sold later, must be explicit and priced accordingly.

Offline/local-first features may continue to work during outages depending on prior caching and browser support.

## 12. Acceptable use

Prohibit at least:

- security attacks;
- scraping intended to reconstruct proprietary course databases where unlawful or contractually prohibited;
- automated account creation/abuse;
- payment fraud;
- harassment through future community/AI features;
- attempts to bypass paid entitlement controls.

Do not prohibit normal personal study exports/backups that the product itself provides.

## 13. Intellectual property

Clarify:

- product code/design/content owned or licensed by `[CONTROLLER]`;
- third-party/open-source components remain under their own licenses;
- user-created notes remain the user's content;
- learner grants only the minimum licence needed to store/sync/process their notes;
- institution custom-content rights defined in order form.

## 14. Privacy

The Privacy Notice forms part of product use. Avoid duplicating detailed data-processing rules in Terms where possible.

## 15. Changes / termination

Define:

- material terms notice;
- service discontinuation process;
- export window where practical;
- termination for abuse/non-payment;
- what happens to learning data after cancellation/deletion.

## 16. Governing law / disputes

To be completed based on final legal entity and target markets.

Netherlands-first consumer launch should be reviewed for mandatory Dutch/EU consumer rights; terms cannot contract out of mandatory protections.

## Publication gate

Do not publish final Terms until:

- legal entity confirmed;
- brand/domain confirmed;
- Free/Plus/Pro features fixed enough to describe truthfully;
- payment and cancellation flows implemented;
- VAT/accounting position reviewed;
- DPA/institution terms separated;
- consumer law review completed for the launch markets.
