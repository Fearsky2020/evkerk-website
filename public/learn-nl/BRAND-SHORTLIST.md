# Independent Brand Shortlist

Status: preliminary naming screen. This is **not** a legal trademark clearance and **not** a final live domain-availability confirmation.

## Naming criteria

The brand must:

- work beyond Chinese-language users;
- sound credible in a municipality, school or employer presentation;
- be easy to pronounce for Dutch and international users;
- avoid words such as church / kerk / Chinese / inburgering that would narrow the product;
- remain usable for B2C, B2B and B2G;
- be visually simple enough for an app icon and wordmark;
- avoid sounding like an official Dutch-government service;
- ideally support a clean `.nl` and defensive `.com` registration.

## Current shortlist

### 1. TAALVIA — preferred working candidate

Idea: `taal` (language) + `via` (way / route / through).

Why it works:

- Immediately has a Dutch-language signal without saying "Dutch school".
- `via` fits the product idea: many native/support languages are different routes into Dutch.
- Sounds acceptable as consumer software and as an institutional platform.
- Easy wordmark: `TAALVIA`.
- Tagline pairing works naturally: `Taalvia — Learn Dutch in your language.`
- Can later support institutional naming such as `Taalvia for Schools`, `Taalvia for Employers`, `Taalvia for Municipalities`.

Risks / checks remaining:

- Final live `.nl` and `.com` RDAP/registrar check immediately before purchase.
- BOIP exact and similarity search in relevant Nice classes before trademark filing.
- EUIPO similarity search before wider EU rollout.
- Check social handles only after domain ownership to avoid unnecessary name squatting.

Preliminary open-web scan: no obvious active Dutch education/software brand under the exact `Taalvia` name was found. This is only an initial screen.

Score: **9/10**.

### 2. TAALVA

Idea: a short coined brand with a clear `taal` root.

Why it works:

- Short and visually strong.
- Easy to place on an app icon and institutional materials.
- Not locked to one learner language.
- Preliminary searches did not surface an obvious Dutch education company with the exact name.

Weaknesses:

- The `va` ending has no immediate meaning to users, so the brand needs a tagline from day one.
- Some unrelated usernames/content use `taalva` online, so it is not as semantically clean as Taalvia.

Score: **8/10**.

### 3. TAALIVO

Idea: `taal` + a lively, international-sounding ending.

Why it works:

- Brandable and easy to pronounce.
- Suitable for app/store-style presentation.
- Does not imply an official school or government service.
- More internationally neutral than a literal Dutch compound word.

Weaknesses:

- More startup-like and less self-explanatory for institutional buyers.
- Requires the descriptor `multilingual Dutch learning platform` consistently during early sales.

Score: **7.5/10**.

## Recommendation

Use **TAALVIA** as the working first-choice candidate until the final live domain + BOIP check is completed.

Suggested brand system if cleared:

- Brand: `Taalvia`
- Descriptor: `Multilingual Dutch learning`
- English promise: `Learn Dutch in your language.`
- Dutch institutional descriptor: `Meertalig platform voor praktisch Nederlands.`
- Chinese descriptor: `用你熟悉的语言，学习真正用得上的荷兰语。`

Potential product families:

- Taalvia Learn
- Taalvia Work
- Taalvia Schools
- Taalvia Partners

Avoid product-family proliferation at launch; initially just use `Taalvia`.

## Domain purchase checklist

Before payment:

1. Check `taalvia.nl` through SIDN Whois/RDAP or a registrar.
2. Check `taalvia.com` through ICANN/Verisign RDAP or a registrar.
3. If both are free and inexpensive, register both at once.
4. Turn on auto-renew.
5. Use registrant privacy where available and appropriate.
6. Enable DNSSEC when the registrar/Cloudflare setup supports it.
7. Do not let the old church path become a second independently indexed copy; redirect/canonicalize once the brand domain is public.

## Trademark checklist

Before filing a Benelux trademark:

1. BOIP exact-word search.
2. BOIP similarity search for visually/phonetic similar marks.
3. Search at least the likely software/education/service classes with professional advice if filing.
4. EUIPO search before broader EU marketing.
5. Do not use `®` until a mark is actually registered.

## Decision gate

Do **not** rename all runtime code yet.

First obtain the domain. After domain ownership is confirmed:

- create the brand wordmark/icon;
- move product-facing page metadata from church identity to brand identity;
- add privacy/terms pages under the brand;
- make the brand domain canonical;
- keep `evkerk.nl` as a referral/redirect path.
